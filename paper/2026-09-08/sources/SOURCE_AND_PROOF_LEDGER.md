## F. What the second pass changed

The second algebraic pass did not merely repeat the source summaries. It:

- corrected “non-Abelian curvature is invisible on the Grassmannian” to the precise statement that the scalar trace QGT can lose it;
- distinguished a graph/frame projector from a selected spectral projector and from the physical vacuum projector;
- exposed the restricted graph family's perturbative limitation rather than identifying it with all Yang–Mills configurations;
- turned Schur positivity into quantitative energy bounds;
- showed by exact examples that local gaps and projector persistence do not imply a uniform vacuum gap;
- made the required physical time/cutoff normalization explicit;
- retained the positivity restriction on proposed topological exclusions.

This is the point of restarting from the newer work rather than changing the numerical prefactor in the 2025 formula.
